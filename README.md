
AstraR6

AstraR6 is an advanced, Python-powered training companion and input automation suite designed specifically for Rainbow Six Siege. Developed for competitive players, AstraR6 bridges the gap between raw mechanics and flawless execution by offering deep macro customization, automation profile switching, and specialized mechanical training modules.
Whether you are looking to master complex mechanical sequences, perfect your positioning, or streamline your practice routine, AstraR6 provides a lightweight, undetectable, and highly customizable environment to elevate your gameplay.

Key Features


Input & Macro Customization

• Dynamic Macro Engine: Create and execute highly customized input sequences with millisecond-precision delays.
• Profile Management: Set up individual configurations for different operators, weapons, or playstyles and switch between them instantly using hotkeys.
• Humanized Delays: Built-in randomized micro-delays prevent mechanical predictability, ensuring smoother system input simulation.
• Multi-Key Binding: Bind complex multi-key combinations to a single input for seamless movement and utility deployment.

Siege Training Utilities

• Mechanical Drill Modules: Integrated timers and reaction modules designed to build muscle memory and sharpen your tracking.
• Warm-up Helper: Automated routines to prepare your hands and reflexes before jumping into ranked queues.
• Overlay Mode: Optional transparent on-screen indicators keep you informed of your active profile without breaking your immersion.

Technical Performance

• Zero Overhead: Written entirely in optimized Python, ensuring the background process leaves your CPU and GPU fully dedicated to maximizing your in-game frame rate.
• Modular Codebase: Clean, structured code makes it easy to write your own plug-ins or extend functionality.
